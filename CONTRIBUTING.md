# Contributing

EmotionVector is a closed research archive with carefully bounded claims.
Contributions are welcome when they preserve the evidence and licensing model.

## Before proposing a change

- Read `README.md`, `REPRODUCIBILITY.md`, and `DATA_LICENSES.md`.
- Keep representation evidence separate from intervention evidence.
- Do not reuse the consumed held-out test for tuning, selection, or a new claim.
- Do not present a mock, heuristic, or LLM Judge as independent human evidence.
- Preserve negative results and protocol deviations.

## Validation

Run the public contract checks documented in `REPRODUCIBILITY.md`.

## Data and artifacts

Do not submit model weights, raw third-party datasets, private review sheets,
condition keys, credentials, local databases, or large runtime artifacts.
Every new dataset or evaluation artifact must record its provenance, license,
split ownership, and claim boundary.

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, contributions intentionally submitted to
this repository are accepted under Apache-2.0, subject to any third-party terms
that apply to included material.
